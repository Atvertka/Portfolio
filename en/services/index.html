FAQ

How does the process work?

We have a short call or chat, go through the task and what the output should look like. Then I send a proposal for format and timeline for that specific scope. No universal price list — tasks vary too much.

What's the timeline?

Depends on the format: a one-off creative is faster, a full AI product takes longer since it includes architecture and testing. The timeline is fixed at the proposal stage, not after the project starts.

Do you work under NDA?

Yes, if the client needs it — I sign before discussing project details.

Will you take on just part of the work (e.g. only the pipeline, no production)?

Yes. It can be full end-to-end or scoped — for example, setting up the pipeline while the client's own team runs it going forward.

Let's discuss your project?

Describe what you need — I'll reply within a day and tell you honestly whether I'm the right fit and what timeline is realistic.
