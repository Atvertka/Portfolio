GustBot — AI Market Intelligence for Telegram

A Telegram bot and media project that replaces hours of monitoring financial news with a single channel: AI analyzes the market in real time and delivers a structured takeaway in seconds.

The problem

Traders and investors physically can't keep up with dozens of sources at once — markets move faster than a person can read and cross-reference the news. The goal was a tool that takes the routine off your hands and surfaces only what actually moves the price.

What the bot does

AI market analysis

Tracks crypto, gold, oil, forex and macroeconomics in real time and turns the news flow into a short, clear takeaway — what happened and why it matters.

Technical analysis

For key assets (BTC, ETH, gold, oil, S&P 500, dollar index) it computes RSI, EMA, MACD, support/resistance levels and produces a signal: bullish / bearish / neutral.

Statement monitoring

Separately tracks public statements from key market figures — central bank heads, major investors, regulators — with an assessment of their likely impact on assets.

Price alerts

The user sets a condition ("tell me when gold breaks this level") — the bot watches the price and notifies automatically.

FLUX channel

The bot fully autonomously runs a connected Telegram channel: daily market briefs, a weekly geopolitical digest, and breakdowns of key events — with zero human involvement in publishing.

Architecture & engineering decisions

Designed and built the system solo — from architecture to production.

01
Data processing pipeline

A multi-stage pipeline: market data collection → normalization and deduplication → AI processing → publishing. Each stage is isolated, so a failure at one step doesn't take down the whole chain.

02
Task-specific prompt engineering

Each topic — crypto, gold, macro, geopolitics — gets its own LLM role/persona with its own style and analytical focus, instead of one universal prompt for everything. A separate prompt pipeline was designed for regular channel content so topics don't repeat day to day.

03
Background task orchestration

A dozen independent recurring jobs (from checking alerts every 5 minutes to generating content once a day) run on a single scheduler without blocking each other or the bot's main message-handling loop.

04
Fault tolerance

The core question for any AI product in production: "what if the model gets it wrong?" An automatic fallback handles malformed AI text at publish time — the system doesn't drop the post, it degrades gracefully and keeps running without manual intervention. Rate-limiting and flood/abuse protection are implemented separately.

05
Monitoring & self-diagnostics

The bot notifies its owner of failures in real time (data source outages, API errors), so issues can be fixed before users ever notice.

Result

GustBot runs in production 24/7, fully automated — from data collection to channel publishing — replacing manual monitoring of dozens of sources with one channel and a ready-made takeaway. The architecture is built to withstand unpredictable AI output and data-source outages without manual intervention.
